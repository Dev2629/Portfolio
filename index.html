ABOUT ME

I am a Ph.D. student at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), working in System Security. My research interests focus on robotics and cyber-physical system security, with a particular interest in securing robotic systems. I am also interested in confidential computing, parallel computing, and compilers, and enjoy exploring how systems can be designed to be both secure and efficient.
